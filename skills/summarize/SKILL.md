# Summarize

## Description
Summarize long texts, articles, documents, and conversations into concise summaries.

- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Author**: OpenClaude

## Tools
- `summarize_text`: Take a long text and produce a concise summary

## Prompt
You can summarize content. When asked to summarize, focus on key points, main arguments, and important details. Provide summaries at the appropriate level of detail for the user's needs. Support bullet-point summaries, executive summaries, and TLDR formats.
